# Hi, I'm Fabian

I'm a GIS and Remote Sensing specialist at the University of Iceland, where I link the IT department and Earth Sciences. A lot of my work is about helping researchers process geospatial data of any kind on the university HPC systems, and building tools that make that easier.

I'm also doing a second master's, in Geophysics. Before that I spent more than five years working hands-on with geospatial equipment and software, and I built GIS tools that take the repetitive work out of different tasks where this is possible.

## What I'm working on

* **Esri on Linux HPC.** Setting up ArcGIS Enterprise (ArcGIS Server, Image Server and Notebook Server) to process and analyse drone data.
* **Research data platform.** Building a map-based platform that helps Earth scientists in Iceland find and preview research data.

## Earlier work

* **Geospatial equipment and software.** Three years in geomatics, preparing, testing and configuring equipment, training users and giving technical support. I worked with everything from software to drones, GNSS receivers, total stations, mobile and static lidar scanners, 360° cameras, ground penetrating radar and more.
* **Cadaster automation with ArcPy.** ArcMap toolboxes for Romanian land registration work: converting e-Terra CGXML files to shapefiles, merging thousands of layers, pulling owner and deed data into one table, batch DXF export, spatial joins, area calculations and fixes for common Generare CG errors. Developed for real data from three rural communes in Gorj County, the tools cut processing time by more than 90%. This was my MSc thesis at West University of Timișoara (2025).
